# Balancing Act: Supplementary Learning Modules — Part 2: The Professor

These modules preserve the comprehensive, step-by-step instructional tutorials, spreadsheets, and hands-on modeling exercises from the first edition of *Balancing Act* (Chapters 5–8). 

While the second edition monograph focuses on high-level architecture, executive through-lines, and the AI forcing function, these supplementary modules serve as the **interactive laboratory and student reference materials** on [LedgerLearning.com](https://ledgerlearning.com).

---

## Module Index

### 1. [Module 2.1: Bookkeeping Mechanics and the Accounting Cycle](./module-2-1-bookkeeping-mechanics-and-accounting-cycle.md)
* **First Edition Source:** Chapter 5 (*Accounting*)
* **Content:** Detailed family finance walkthrough, credit counseling scenario, T-accounts, debit/credit mechanics, trial balances, and closing entries.
* **Audience/Use:** Fundamentals course for software engineers and systems architects learning double-entry accounting mechanics.

### 2. [Module 2.2: Business Events and Flexible Income Statements](./module-2-2-business-events-and-flexible-statements.md)
* **First Edition Source:** Chapter 6 (*Business Events*)
* **Content:** Deep-dive into general ledger balances versus journal entry repositories, demonstrating how to generate custom-period financial statements without month-end closing routines.
* **Audience/Use:** Intermediate lab on event storage vs. balance pre-aggregation.

### 3. [Module 2.3: Single-Sided vs. Double-Sided Recording in Modern Systems](./module-2-3-single-sided-vs-double-sided-recording.md)
* **First Edition Source:** Chapter 7 (*Resources and Agents*)
* **Content:** Analysis of single-sided recording, checkbook registers, automated offset accounting (Quicken model), and comparing 55-row double-entry systems to 31-row event systems.
* **Audience/Use:** Subledger design and transaction-capture optimization.

### 4. [Module 2.4: Hands-On REAL Modeling Lab (Car Maintenance Case Study)](./module-2-4-real-modeling-and-car-maintenance-lab.md)
* **First Edition Source:** Chapter 8 (*REAL Analysis Method*)
* **Content:** The complete 6-step REAL (Resources, Events, Agents, Locations) business process modeling method, including entity-relationship blueprints, cardinality tables, and SQL/database mapping for the Car Maintenance Information System.
* **Audience/Use:** Hands-on data modeling workshop and systems analysis curriculum.
