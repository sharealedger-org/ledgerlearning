Courses & Resources

All courses are free. They use a combination of vlog episodes organized by topic, selections from the Balancing Act textbook, and downloadable resources. Feedback and suggestions on additional courses are welcome.

Reclassification Processes Course

The most complete course on the site — the only one currently with assessments. Covers the full lifecycle of reclassification: the problem it solves, the recast view, the reclass view, the switch view, effective-date joins, backdating, and code sets.

Reclassification is one of the most expensive and error-prone processes in financial services. This course explains why it exists and what a better architecture would look like.
