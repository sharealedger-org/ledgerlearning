Customer Profitability: A Conversation with Ewan Puckle Hobbs

December 12, 2017

For the last five years, I've talked about issues I was taught about by Ewan Puckle Hobbs; specifically measurement of major enterprise transformations, or more directly customer profitability. He helped broaden my perspective from simply accounting processes to wider sets of measurement activities. A core part of this problem is capturing cost of customer activities. Capturing revenue by customer is not typically too hard--customer pay us directly, we simply need to keep track of what they have paid us. (This isn't a trivial problem given our systems historically loose these amounts quickly by summarizing them out...but that's a system problem. You can review an earlier episode about the issues here). But costs are not accumulated by customer--we pay all sorts of other people for the services we provide to customers. I think you'll find some interesting insights from Ewan on the issues involved in making our organizations more efficient.
