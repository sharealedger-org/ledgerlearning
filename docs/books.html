Balancing Act: A Practical Approach to Business Event Based Insights

Written from 30 years of financial systems consulting, Balancing Act is a comprehensive guide to understanding why financial systems look the way they do — and what a better architecture would look like. Beginning with the history of bookkeeping and Luca Pacioli, the book walks through computers, accounting theory, REA analysis, data supply chains, GenevaERS, and ends with a concrete plan for financial system renewal.

68 chapters + appendices, organized in 8 parts:

  1. Part 1: The Pearl — The Problem & The Solution
  2. Part 2: The Professor — Computers, Accounting, REA
  3. Part 3: The Partner — Reality, Consulting, Architecture
  4. Part 4: The Projects — 15 chapters on the REAL method
  5. Part 5: The Programmer — GenevaERS views & phases
  6. Part 6: The Platform — GL, Accounting Rules, Go Live
  7. Part 7: The Plan — Finance, Risk, Partnership
  8. Part 8: Appendices — Accounting Model & EDBM

Metric Engine: Reinventing the Data Supply Chains for Business

A technical white paper and monograph on high-performance reporting engines — the concept of a "metric engine" as an analogue to a search engine, but for business measurement. Rather than building reports on demand from raw data, a metric engine pre-summarizes against all possible keys, making any report instantaneous.

Covers the architecture of pre-hierarchy event repositories, step-up summarization, temporal event handling, and how systems like GenevaERS implement metric engine concepts at enterprise scale — processing millions of ledger balance updates per second.
