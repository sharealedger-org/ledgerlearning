Allocation Processing Steps

October 29, 2019

This week's episode shows the general steps of an allocation processes, from transactions to balances and back down to transactions. This graphic gives a sense of how the data flows for an allocation process. Steps of an allocation process
  1. The costs must be accumulated to a total costs.
  2. The activities or drivers must also be accumulated to get to a total number of activities.
  3. The total costs are divided by the total activities to get to a per unit cost,
  4. Which is then multiplied by each of the driver specifics to determine what the cost of that driver is.
