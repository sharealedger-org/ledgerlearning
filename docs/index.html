The Problem with Financial Systems

In April 2018, Kip Twitchell led an impromptu 57-minute working session with IBM engineers in Bangalore — building the entire argument from scratch using nothing but personal spending data and a whiteboard. No assumed knowledge. Real questions from the room.

Why can't your bank show you five years of line-item history? Why do banks spend hours every night "posting"? Why can reconciliation never be fully automated? Why might 30% of financial services operating costs be people running computers backwards?
