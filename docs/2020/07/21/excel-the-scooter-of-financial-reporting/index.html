Excel: The Scooter of Financial Reporting

July 21, 2020

In a number of my videos I've been on different modes of transportation, including a plane, a train, a taxi, a bus, a bike, and even an ill-fate attempt to video while walking.

Today's video is while riding a scooter.

A scooter is the most nimble yet quick form of transportation out of all of them I've recorded on. In financial reporting, a spreadsheet is the most nimble form of financial reporting application there is.

This nimbleness and low cost goes a long ways to explaining the ubiquity of spreadsheet use for all kinds of metric analysis. It deals with structure data; it accumulates and calculates very effectively. It is the most widely used financial application there is.
